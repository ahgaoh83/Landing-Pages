Our Story

Four things we refuse to compromise on

We started Ember & Origin because the most interesting part of a bag of coffee — the person who grew it — kept getting reduced to a country name on the back label.

I

Human Provenance

Every bag carries a real name, a real farm, a real story. We don't sell origins — we introduce people.

II

Radical Transparency

Roast profiles, processing methods, and farmer economics — visible on the page, not buried in a blog post.

III

Intentional Craft

Small-batch means we can say no to commodity. Every roast decision is deliberate, never scaled for volume.

IV

Earned Trust

We build community through education and honesty — not discount codes and manufactured urgency.

Radical Transparency

The numbers most roasters don't publish

If we're going to charge more, you should be able to see exactly where it goes.

What we pay
Above the Fair Trade base for every lot, negotiated directly with the farm or cooperative — never through a commodity desk.
Batch size
Under 12kg per roast. Small enough that a single roast is a decision, not a schedule.
Roast to ship
48 hours. We roast on Mondays and Thursdays, and nothing sits in a warehouse waiting for a sales cycle.
Sourcing
Every origin is visited before it's bought. GPS coordinates and cooperative details are printed on the story card.
What we don't do
No commodity blends. No stock photography. No countdown timers. No discounts that quietly come out of a farmer's price.
